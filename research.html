Research Interests

My research interests span from molecules to organisms, and I aim to probe evolutionary and ecological processes mechanistically through large-scale experiments. Throughout my undergraduate research, I noticed that I was always curious about questions like this:

Why did ATPases come to have eight subunits?

I came to see that answering such why-questions requires understanding the genotype-phenotype-fitness landscape. With the generous support and mentorship of Dr. Yeonwoo Park, I began exploring one part of this landscape by studying how molecular phenotypes evolve, focusing on molecular system drift in microRNA processing.

Leading my first project over the past year, I learned that there were a bunch of interesting things that I could investigate in the next few years, hopefully as a PhD student.
