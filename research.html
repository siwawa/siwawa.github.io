Research Interests

My research interests span from molecules to organisms, and I aim to probe evolutionary and ecological processes mechanistically through large-scale experiments. Throughout my undergraduate research, I noticed that I was always curious in: Why [something] had not evolved, and I came to see that answering such questions requires understanding the genotype-phenotype-fitness landscape. With the generous support and mentorship of Dr. Yeonwoo Park, I began exploring one part of this landscape by studying how molecular phenotypes evolve, focusing on molecular system drift in microRNA processing. Leading my first project over the past year, I learned that there were a bunch of interesting things that I could investigate the next few years, hopefully as a PhD candidate.
