Research Experience

Center for RNA ResearchFeb 2025 to present

Advised by Prof. V. Narry Kim and Dr. Yeonwoo Park

  • Leading a systematic study of how microRNA processing evolves across jawed vertebrates, with a focus on system drift. Reconstructed historical changes and inferred selection on their phenotypic effects.
  • Building a phylogenetic tree of the TENT family, which has deep homologies across the eukaryotic tree of life.
  • Analyzed high-throughput screens of hundreds of thousands of candidate self-cleaving ribozymes and identified an artifact with troubleshooting experiments.

Laboratory of Computational Structural and Systems BiologyJan to Mar 2024, Aug 2024 to Jan 2025

Advised by Prof. Minkyung Baek

  • Developed a structure-guided MSA augmentation approach using Foldseek to recover structurally related remote homologs for AlphaFold2 protein structure prediction.
  • Proposed a framework for reconstructing viable evolutionary trajectories of viral spike proteins by using computational protein design to exclude structurally unstable intermediates.

Saengjins (Ecology and Evolution Research Society)Mar to Nov 2024

Advised by Prof. Youngsung Joo

  • Tested the hypothesis of adult paper-wasp mimicry in the bean bug (Riptortus pedestris) by quantifying flight posture and acoustic signals, finding insufficient support for adult mimicry.

Laboratory of Population GenomicsJul to Aug 2023

Advised by Prof. Choongwon Jeong

  • Characterized population structure and admixture between domestic chickens and Red Junglefowl.
