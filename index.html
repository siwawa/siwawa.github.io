Hi! I’m a final-year undergraduate in the School of Biological Sciences at Seoul National University, working with Dr. Yeonwoo Park in Prof. V. Narry Kim’s lab. I want to understand ecology and evolution as a mechanistic process, using large-scale experiments, experimental evolution, and possibly deep learning.
